**Nia:** Our policy engine can require a plant sponsor, set an expiration date, and send removal evidence back to the identity platform.

**Victor:** We would need evidence for our security team before procurement considers a pilot. Can you provide architecture and penetration-test materials?

**Nia:** Yes. I will send the security architecture, data-flow diagram, and latest penetration-test summary tomorrow, November fourth.

**Victor:** Send them to victor dot chen at atlas m f g dot com. I will introduce you to our procurement manager by Friday, November sixth.

**Nia:** I can also prepare a sample contractor policy after you confirm which identity provider the plants use.

**Victor:** I will confirm the identity provider in my reply. Please do not send a commercial proposal yet; security review comes first.

**Nia:** Understood. We can discuss pilot scope only after the security team reviews the package.

**Victor:** Good. I will make the procurement introduction Friday, but that introduction does not mean the pilot is approved.
