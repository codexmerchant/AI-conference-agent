## Script

**Owen:** Hello, I am Owen Brooks, Research Lead at BrightPath Learning. Your poster on student confidence caught my attention.

**Owen:** BrightPath logs hint requests, response changes, and confidence checks. We want an independent study of whether those signals predict learning gains.

**Elena:** We could explore that, but the study would need a clear consent process and strict limits on student identifiers.

**Owen:** Agreed. We can provide de-identified event data and keep school names out of the research export.

**Elena:** How many classrooms would be available for an initial study?

**Owen:** About twelve classrooms across two districts. I will send a draft research protocol and data dictionary tomorrow, October eighth.

**Elena:** Please send them to elena dot varga at horizon dot edu. I will send our institutional review checklist by Monday, October twelfth.

**Owen:** After I receive the checklist, I will revise the consent and retention sections before our next conversation.

**Elena:** Then we should hold a design workshop with one district representative. We can schedule it after both teams review the documents.

**Owen:** Good plan. The workshop is exploratory; neither organization is committing to launch the study yet.

**Elena:** Exactly. I will look for your protocol tomorrow and send the checklist Monday.
