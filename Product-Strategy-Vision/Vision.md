# Agentic Conference Secretary — Product Vision

**Status:** Draft for stakeholder review

**Source:** `PRD_AI_conference_agent.md` and the current epic definitions

**Last updated:** July 23, 2026

## Vision statement

Create a frictionless, context-aware AI assistant that turns real-world conference interactions into persistent, actionable intelligence.

The Agentic Conference Secretary captures conversations, images, contacts, sessions, and surrounding context; organizes them into a durable knowledge and relationship memory; and helps professionals follow up, learn, and improve over time.

## The future we want to create

Conference-heavy professionals should not need to choose between participating fully and documenting what happened. The product should quietly capture approved information, understand its meaning, connect it to people and events, and make the results useful while they are still timely.

After a conference, the user should be able to answer:

- Who did I meet, and why do they matter?
- What did I learn across conversations and sessions?
- Which commitments and follow-ups require action?
- How are people, organizations, topics, and events connected?
- Where did I spend time well, and what should I do differently next time?

Each event should add to a persistent memory rather than becoming another disconnected set of notes.

## Problem

Professionals currently rely on fragmented notes, recordings, photographs, contact applications, calendars, email, and memory. This creates:

- Lost context around contacts and conversations
- Incomplete or delayed follow-up
- Disconnected session notes and media
- Repeated manual organization
- Limited visibility into conference value
- No durable intelligence across events

Existing note-taking, transcription, and CRM tools address parts of this workflow, but they do not provide a unified, context-aware system that learns across real-world interactions.

## Target users

The initial product is intended for conference-heavy professionals, including:

- Venture capitalists and investors
- Executives and operators
- Consultants
- Sales and business-development leaders
- Researchers
- Other professionals whose results depend on relationships, sessions, and follow-through

## Product promise

The product will help users:

1. **Capture with minimal friction** — record approved audio, images, and contextual metadata without disrupting participation.
2. **Understand what happened** — classify interactions, identify speakers and entities, extract topics, and connect media to sessions.
3. **Structure useful knowledge** — turn raw inputs into contacts, conversations, sessions, action items, and relationships.
4. **Act while context is fresh** — generate summaries, reports, and follow-up drafts quickly.
5. **Remember across events** — build searchable semantic memory and a relationship graph that compounds over time.
6. **Improve performance** — reveal patterns, missed opportunities, and recommendations for future conferences.

## Product identity

The Agentic Conference Secretary acts as a:

- Secretary
- Analyst
- Relationship memory
- Lightweight CRM companion
- Knowledge system
- Performance coach

It is not merely a note-taking application, transcription service, or traditional CRM. Its intended category is an **AI-native conference and relationship intelligence platform**.

## Experience principles

### Frictionless

Core capture and retrieval workflows should require minimal user effort.

### Context before content

Outputs should preserve who, what, where, when, and why—not only raw transcripts or images.

### Same-day value

Users should receive useful summaries, action items, and follow-up support while conference context is still current.

### Compounding value

Every approved interaction should improve the user's long-term memory and relationship intelligence.

### Trust by design

Recording consent, visible capture indicators, encryption, access control, retention controls, and traceability must be built into the experience.

### Human control

AI-generated identities, summaries, relationships, and recommendations should expose confidence and remain reviewable and correctable.

## Product ecosystem

- **iPhone/iPad:** low-friction capture and timely prompts
- **Mac/Desktop:** review, editing, analysis, reporting, and export
- **Cloud:** AI processing, synchronization, storage, search, and graph intelligence
- **Integrations:** calendar, communication, contacts, CRM, notes, and storage systems

## Measures of progress

The vision will be demonstrated through improvements in:

- Percentage of meaningful interactions captured
- Accuracy and usefulness of summaries
- Speed and completion of follow-up
- Time saved during post-conference organization
- Daily usage during conferences
- User retention
- User-reported conference ROI

Numerical targets remain **TBD pending stakeholder approval and baseline measurement**.

## Long-term horizon

Potential future extensions include wearables, real-time coaching, team intelligence, enterprise analytics, and conference benchmarking. These opportunities should follow—not distract from—proving reliable capture, useful outputs, user trust, and persistent memory.

## Vision test

The product is moving toward this vision when it becomes increasingly invisible to use, delivers useful results on the same day, and becomes more valuable with every approved interaction.
